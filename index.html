Early Life

The beginning of the CR7 story

From Madeira to the World

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born on 5 February 1985 in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal.

His football journey began in Madeira. He later joined Sporting CP before making his breakthrough at Manchester United.

His development transformed him from a talented young winger into one of football's most prolific goalscorers.

The name CR7 eventually became one of the most recognizable identities in football.

Career Journey

The major chapters of CR7's professional career

Sporting CP

Ronaldo developed through Sporting's academy before making his senior debut.

Manchester United

Ronaldo developed into a global star and won major domestic and European trophies.

Real Madrid

Between 2009 and 2018, Ronaldo scored 451 competitive goals for Real Madrid.

Juventus

Ronaldo continued his goalscoring career in Italy after joining Juventus.

Manchester United Return

Ronaldo returned to Manchester United in 2021.

Al-Nassr

Ronaldo joined Al-Nassr in January 2023 and continued his career in Saudi Arabia.
